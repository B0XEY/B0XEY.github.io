Gameplay

Core systems

01

Open Solar System

Explore an entire solar system of unique planets, each with their own terrain, atmosphere, and resources. No loading screens, fly between worlds freely.

02

Resource Gathering

Mine minerals, harvest compounds, and collect rare materials from across different worlds. Every planet holds something you'll need to progress.

03

Crafting & Building

Use gathered materials to craft tools, repair ship components, and build outposts.

04

Survival Systems

Manage power, oxygen. Each planet has its own hazards like toxic atmospheres, extreme temperatures, and unstable terrain.

05

Procedural Worlds

Every planet is shaped by procedural noise for unique terrain, biomes, and resource distribution. No two playthroughs are identical.

06

Find a Home

Somewhere in this solar system is a way back. Chart each planet, piece together what happened, and work toward an answer
